# Explanation

Explanation pages are **discussion**. They step back from the work to give context,
background, and reasons: *why* things are the way they are, what alternatives were
considered, and how the parts relate. They support understanding rather than action.

!!! abstract "What belongs here"
    - Design rationale, trade-offs, history, and connections between ideas.
    - Opinion and perspective, clearly argued.
    - Discussion that a reader could enjoy away from the keyboard.

!!! failure "What does not belong here"
    - Instructions of any kind → [Tutorials](../tutorials/index.md) or [How-to guides](../how-to/index.md)
    - Lists of facts to look up → [Reference](../reference/index.md)
