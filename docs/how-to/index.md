# How-to guides

How-to guides are **recipes**. Each one takes a reader who already knows what they want
through the steps to get it done. A guide serves someone *working*; it does not teach the
basics.

!!! abstract "What belongs here"
    - A title that names a goal: "How to…" or an imperative such as "Run the black-box tests".
    - Steps for a real-world task, including the conditions or variations that matter.
    - The assumption that the reader is competent, with no teaching detours.

!!! failure "What does not belong here"
    - Step-by-step learning for beginners → [Tutorials](../tutorials/index.md)
    - Complete descriptions of options and formats → [Reference](../reference/index.md)
    - Why things are the way they are → [Explanation](../explanation/index.md)
